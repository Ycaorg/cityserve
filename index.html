Who We Are

CityServe Hub PH is an online multi-service platform designed to connect customers with service providers.

Instead of contacting different providers separately, customers can use CityServe to discover, request, and coordinate services in one convenient platform.

Our Mission

To provide customers with convenient, accessible, and quality service options through a simple digital platform while creating opportunities for service providers.

Our Vision

To become a trusted multi-service platform that makes everyday service needs easier for customers and helps service providers reach more customers.
